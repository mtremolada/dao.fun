# Solana Launchpad Landscape (as of 2026-08) — Mechanics, Product Surface, Infrastructure

## 1. Platform mechanics comparison

### pump.fun (dominant incumbent, ~46-77% market share depending on week)
- **Curve**: Constant-product AMM with **virtual reserves** (x*y=k). Initialized at ~30 virtual SOL and 1.073B virtual tokens (raw units: 30 SOL / 1,073T raw with 6 decimals). Real token reserves on curve: 793.1M tokens; 206.9M reserved for migration liquidity. Total supply 1B, 6 decimals. Initial price ≈ 0.000000028 SOL/token.
- **Graduation**: Curve completes when all ~793.1M curve tokens are sold ≈ 85-86 SOL net buys ≈ ~$69k market cap. Migration is instant and free since March 2025: ~85 SOL + ~206.9M unsold tokens seed a canonical **PumpSwap** pool (pool index 0); LP tokens minted then burned (liquidity permanently locked); a fixed ~0.015 SOL network fee is deducted. Before March 2025 graduation went to **Raydium** with a 6 SOL migration fee — clones copying old articles get this wrong.
- **Fees (evolved over time — important)**: originally 1% flat on curve trades. May 13, 2025: creator fee share added ("creator revenue sharing") on curve + PumpSwap trades. PumpSwap base: ~0.25% swap (≈0.20% LP / 0.05% protocol) plus creator slice. **Sept 2, 2025 "Project Ascend"**: dynamic creator fees by market cap — ~0.95%/trade creator take for $88k-$300k MC tokens, tapering to 0.05% at $20M+ MC; fees computed by an external "Pump Fees Program" so the schedule is upgradable. Creators claimed $19M+ in the first weeks. Platform revenue: $321M (2024), $664M (2025), ~$98M (Q1 2026).
- **Supply convention**: fixed 1B supply, 6 decimals, mint authority held/revoked by program (no inflation), no freeze authority, metadata via Metaplex CPI at create, mint address vanity-suffixed "pump".

### Raydium LaunchLab / LetsBonk (bonk.fun) (~8-42% share, peaked >50% mid-2025)
- **Curve**: LaunchLab is a configurable launch framework; supports multiple curve formulas (constant-product-style and quadratic price(s)=k·(s/S_max)²; docs mention linear/exponential shape options). "JustSendIt" mode = pump.fun-compatible defaults: 1B supply, 793.1M on curve, 85 SOL graduation. Custom mode: configurable quote mint (SOL/USDC/RAY), graduation threshold, fee shares, vesting, LP policy (burn / lock / creator-received).
- **Graduation**: at 85 SOL quote collected → **Raydium CPMM** pool (Token-2022 launches must use CPMM, not AMM v4). Post-grad fee = CPMM config, typically 0.25%.
- **Fees**: 1% on curve trades, split platform/protocol/creator (creator share configurable; LetsBonk gives creators up to 0.10%). LetsBonk allocates ~30-58% of platform fees to open-market **BONK buyback + burn**, ~30% to BONKsol validator, rest to ops — the "ecosystem token buyback" model that Heaven later maximized.

### Meteora DBC — the white-label curve engine (powers Believe, Virtuals-on-Solana, Bags, Jupiter Studio, Moonit's Meteora path)
- **Curve**: fully configurable — up to 16-20 liquidity distribution points/price ranges allowing constant-product, linear, exponential, or exotic shapes; quote token SOL or USDC; SPL or Token-2022; decimals 6-9; configurable supply. Config stored in a partner "config key".
- **Fees**: base fee + optional **fee scheduler** (decaying anti-snipe fee), optional dynamic fee, rate limiter, configurable creator trading fee %, collected in quote-only or both tokens.
- **Graduation**: at a configured migration quote threshold, a keeper/"migrator service" moves liquidity to **DAMM v1 or DAMM v2** (fee tiers 0.25%-6% or custom); LP split between partner/creator with locked/unlocked portions configurable.
- **Believe**: DBC-based, ~$100k MC graduation to Meteora; 2% transaction fee split 50/50 creator/platform; dynamic launch fee starts extremely high to punish snipers; launch-by-replying-on-X flow; creators claim by linking their X account.
- **Virtuals (Solana)**: pay 100 VIRTUAL to launch; curve denominated in VIRTUAL; graduates at 42,000 VIRTUAL accumulated → Meteora pool; LP locked 10 years.

### Moonshot by DEX Screener → rebranded **Moonit** (early 2025)
- **Curve**: two types — **Classic** (accelerating price; migrates when ~80% of 1B supply sold) and **Flat** (fixed price until ~49% sold, needs ≥25 SOL liquidity, supports vesting allocations).
- **Graduation**: ~$75k MC → 88 SOL + 200M tokens to **Meteora** pool (LP permanently locked) or Raydium AMM v4 (LP burned + 2 SOL bonding bonus). Graduates get free Enhanced Token Info on DEX Screener and listing across Axiom/Photon/Jupiter.
- **Fees**: 1% on all trades; LP-reward split 80% creator / 20% platform (SOL side); milestone buybacks at $5M/$20M/$50M/$100M MC route 10/20/50/100% of LP fees to automated token buybacks.

### Boop.fun (Apr 2025, by dingaling)
- **Curve**: bonding curve with ~75% of supply on curve; graduation at ~400 SOL raised (much higher bar than pump.fun).
- **Post-grad**: 2% fee, partially redistributed; graduated "cults" distribute daily **BOOP airdrops** — 10% creator / 90% holders; BOOP stakers get 60% of trading fees + 5% of each graduated token's supply. Differentiator = staking/airdrop flywheel rather than pure curve.

### Bags (bags.fm, May 2025) (~6% share early 2026)
- Built on **Meteora DBC**. Differentiator = **shared creator royalties**: launcher can route up to 90% of creator fees to other people (e.g. the meme's originator, an influencer's X handle) keeping ≥10%; "fee splits to any X account" is the headline feature. $21M+ creator earnings claimed; ~3,900 launches/day peaks in early 2026.

### Heaven (heaven.xyz, Aug 2025)
- **No bonding curve**: every token launches directly on Heaven's own AMM seeded with ~35 virtual SOL ("virtual liquidity floor") — i.e., virtual reserves without a graduation event at all. Fee 1% below $100k MC, tapering to 0.25% (community coins) / 0.5% (creator coins) above. Creator classes: Creator (1% fee in SOL), Community (0.1%), Blocked (fees diverted to buybacks). "God Flywheel": 100% of protocol revenue buys back and burns LIGHT. Peaked at 15-28% launch share Aug 2025; LIGHT fell from $130M to ~$33M MC within weeks — hype-spike trajectory.

### Jupiter Studio (Apr 2025, on Meteora DBC)
- Meme mode: seeded ~$16k starting MC, fixed ~$69k graduation → Meteora. Custom mode: quote token, market caps, creator vesting, anti-sniping. **Anti-sniper fee: starts 99%, decays to 0% over randomized 15-60s**, credited to creator, on top of 1% trade fee. Min raise to graduate ≈ 15,000 USDC-equivalent. 0.04 SOL launch cost. ~1.8% share.

### 2026 status
No major *new* mechanic entrant in 2026; the market consolidated around pump.fun (~46%) vs LetsBonk (~42%) with Bags/JupStudio/Believe splitting the rest. The 2025-2026 meta-shifts to copy: (1) creator fee share is now table stakes, (2) dynamic/decaying anti-snipe fees, (3) market-cap-tiered fees (Ascend, Heaven), (4) ecosystem-token buyback/burn flywheels, (5) owning your own graduation AMM (PumpSwap, Heaven) instead of gifting flow to Raydium.

## 2. Product surface of a pump.fun-class site

**Board/list page**: token grid/list sorted by *newest / featured (trending algorithm) / market cap / 24h volume / recently bumped*; "**King of the Hill**" hero slot (pump.fun: token crossing ~45 SOL ≈ halfway up curve with recent velocity takes a spotlight box); live "bump" animations — cards flash/re-sort on each trade; ticking "created Xs ago"; NSFW toggle; search. The 2025-2026 pro-trader convention (Axiom "Pulse", Photon, pump.fun Advanced, BullX) is a **three-column lifecycle layout: New Pairs → Final Stretch (about to graduate, e.g. >75-90% curve) → Migrated/Graduated**, each streaming in real time with quick-buy buttons and filter chips (min MC, holder count, dev-holding %, socials-present).

**Coin page**: candlestick chart (TradingView charting library w/ custom datafeed; MC and price denominations); buy/sell trade panel (SOL amount presets, slippage setting, quote preview from curve state, priority-fee setting); **bonding-curve progress bar** with % and "graduates at $69k" copy (progress = (793.1M − curve_token_balance…)/793.1M); King-of-the-hill progress; holder list (top 20 with % of supply, dev/bonding-curve/sniper badges); live trades feed (buys green/sells red, wallet, SOL amount, token amount, time, tx link); threaded comments with images (off-chain DB, wallet-signed); creator info + socials; livestream module (pump.fun native livestreams — pulled Nov 2024, relaunched 2025 with moderation). 

**Launch form**: name, ticker, description, image/video upload → IPFS, optional twitter/telegram/website, optional initial dev buy (with warning), ~free create (creator pays rent + optional dev buy; some platforms 0.02-0.04 SOL fee), vanity mint suffix. **Profile pages**: username/avatar, coins created, coins held, replies, followers, PnL on some platforms; creator-fee claim dashboard ("claim your earnings") is now a core retention surface.

## 3. Data infrastructure powering live feeds

Standard pattern (used by pump.fun clones and trading terminals):
- **Ingest**: subscribe to the launchpad program via **Yellowstone Geyser gRPC** (lowest latency, e.g. Helius LaserStream with auto-reconnect + ~24h historical replay) or Enhanced WebSockets (`logsSubscribe`/`blockSubscribe`) or **Helius Webhooks** for lower-volume needs. Decode Anchor events by 8-byte discriminator (CreateEvent, TradeEvent, CompleteEvent/migration).
- **Index**: write decoded creates/trades/graduations to Postgres (+ Redis for hot state: current curve reserves, KOTH, trending scores). WebSocket streams are live-only — historical backfill requires replaying blocks or a third-party indexer (Bitquery/Substreams/Carbon).
- **Serve**: REST API for board queries/coin detail + **server WebSocket fan-out** to browsers (new-coin events, per-coin trade channels, board bump events). pump.fun itself runs `frontend-api` REST + WS; PumpPortal exists as a third-party mirror of this pattern.
- Public RPC is insufficient for holder snapshots at scale (gPA rate limits/index exclusions) — use Helius DAS/keyed RPC (this matches the repo's own D-026 finding).

## 4. OHLCV/candles
Three approaches in the wild: (a) **self-indexed candles** — aggregate your own TradeEvent stream into 1s/1m/5m/1h buckets (open=first, high/low=max/min, close=last, volume=sum) and expose a TradingView **UDF/custom datafeed**; this is what serious launchpads do since their tokens don't exist anywhere else pre-graduation; (b) third-party candle APIs (Birdeye OHLCV V3, Bitquery pre-aggregated OHLC down to 1-second, Codex) wired into the TradingView charting library; (c) lazy embed of DexScreener/GeckoTerminal iframe — only works **post-graduation** (embeds don't see your custom curve), so an MVP still needs (a) or (b) for the bonding phase. TradingView's Advanced Charts library is free but requires application/approval; lightweight-charts is the no-approval fallback.

## 5. Token metadata
Convention: at create, CPI to **Metaplex mpl-token-metadata** (name, symbol, uri, creators; immutable preferred) in the same tx that initializes the curve; image + JSON (name/symbol/description/image/socials, `showName` flag) pinned to **IPFS** — Pinata is the de-facto pinning service in every current guide (pump.fun closed its once-public `pump.fun/api/ipfs` upload endpoint to third parties; NFT.storage/Irys are alternates); serve via your own gateway with fallbacks. **Authority expectations**: mint authority transferred to the curve PDA and effectively revoked at/after graduation (LaunchLab explicitly revokes at graduation; pump.fun supply fixed from mint), **freeze authority null from birth**, metadata immutable — trading terminals surface "Mint Auth revoked / Freeze revoked / LP burned" as safety badges (DEX Screener/RugCheck style), so deviating breaks every downstream safety scanner.

## 6. Graduation UX
- Per-coin **progress bar** ("bonding curve progress: 87%") + explicit copy explaining ~$69k/85 SOL threshold and that remaining liquidity migrates & LP burns.
- Board-level "**about to graduate / Final Stretch**" rail (sorted by curve %), "**recently graduated**" rail with time-since-migration, and post-grad link-out to the AMM pool + aggregator listings (Jupiter routing pickup is automatic for PumpSwap/Meteora/Raydium pools).
- Graduation moment: celebratory state change on coin page ("migrated to PumpSwap"), KOTH slot at ~50% progress as a mid-funnel milestone, and free DEX Screener enhanced listing (Moonit) as a graduation reward pattern.

- MVP curve choice: use a pump.fun-style constant-product curve with virtual reserves (30 virtual SOL / 1.073B virtual tokens, 1B fixed supply at 6 decimals, 793.1M sellable) — it is the ecosystem-standard math that every trading terminal, sniper, and indexer already understands; make graduation threshold and fee bps program config (upgradable via a fees/config account like pump.fun's external Pump Fees Program) rather than hardcoded constants.
- Graduation MVP: migrate at ~85 SOL to an existing battle-tested CPMM (Raydium CPMM or Meteora DAMM v2) with LP burned/locked, exactly one canonical pool, and an idempotent keeper/crank service to execute migration; owning your own AMM (PumpSwap model) is a v2 revenue play, not MVP scope.
- Fee model MVP: 1% curve trade fee with an on-chain creator-fee share and a claim instruction + claim UI — creator revenue share is table stakes in 2026; MC-tiered dynamic fees (Ascend-style) and a decaying anti-snipe launch fee (99%→0% over 15-60s, Jupiter Studio-style) are the two highest-value fast-follows.
- MVP feature checklist — board page: paginated token grid with sort by new/market-cap/volume/recently-bumped, live 'bump' animation on trades, King-of-the-Hill spotlight (~50% curve progress), search, and a three-column lifecycle view (New / Final Stretch >=75-90% curve / Recently Graduated) since that is the 2026 discovery convention.
- MVP feature checklist — coin page: TradingView chart (lightweight-charts or Advanced Charts w/ custom UDF datafeed; price + market-cap denominations), buy/sell panel with SOL presets, slippage, and quote preview computed from live curve reserves, bonding-curve progress bar with graduation copy, top-20 holder list with % and dev/curve badges, live trades feed (side, wallet, amounts, time, tx link), wallet-signed comments (off-chain DB), creator + socials block.
- MVP feature checklist — launch flow: form with name/ticker/description/image (+optional socials), client-side image validation, server-side pin of image + metadata JSON to Pinata (or Irys) with your own gateway + fallback gateways, optional initial dev-buy in the create tx, vanity mint suffix optional; create tx must CPI Metaplex token-metadata, set freeze authority to null at mint, and leave mint authority only with the curve PDA (revoked at graduation) — safety scanners and traders check these.
- MVP feature checklist — profiles & retention: wallet profile (coins created/held, replies), creator fee-claim dashboard showing accrued SOL; skip livestreams, follower graphs, and mobile apps for MVP.
- Standard architecture pattern to adopt: (1) Anchor program emitting CreateEvent/TradeEvent/CompleteEvent; (2) ingest via Yellowstone Geyser gRPC (Helius LaserStream for managed reconnect + 24h replay) with Helius webhooks as a cheaper fallback, decoding events by Anchor discriminator; (3) Postgres indexer tables (tokens, trades, candles, holders, graduations) + Redis hot state (curve reserves, KOTH, trending); (4) REST API for board/coin/candles + one server WebSocket fanning out new-token, per-coin trade, and board-bump channels; (5) Next.js frontend consuming REST + WS with wallet-standard signing of server-built txs (matches this repo's existing D-028 pattern).
- Candles: self-index OHLCV from your own TradeEvent stream (1s/1m/5m/15m/1h/4h/1d buckets, open/high/low/close/volume, carry-forward close for empty buckets) and expose a TradingView-compatible datafeed — third-party APIs (Birdeye/Bitquery) cannot see your custom curve program until you integrate with them, and DexScreener embeds only work post-graduation.
- Holder data: do NOT rely on public RPC getProgramAccounts (rate-limited/index-excluded — matches repo finding D-026); maintain holder balances incrementally from indexed trade events, reconciling periodically via Helius DAS getTokenAccounts.
- Trust/badge surface: display mint-authority-revoked, freeze-authority-none, LP-burned, curve-progress, dev-holding %, and sniper-count badges — these are what Axiom/Photon/DexScreener-conditioned users look for before buying.
- Differentiation levers observed in the market if desired later (not MVP): shared creator royalties to any X handle (Bags), ecosystem-token buyback/burn flywheel (LetsBonk/Heaven), staking + airdrop flywheel for graduated tokens (Boop), MC-tiered fee decay (Ascend/Heaven), higher graduation bar for quality signaling (Boop 400 SOL).
- Mechanics changed materially during 2024-2026 (Raydium→PumpSwap Mar 2025; creator fees May 2025; Ascend dynamic fees Sept 2025; Moonshot→Moonit rebrand) — treat any pre-2025 tutorial numbers (e.g. 6 SOL Raydium migration fee, $12k KOTH) as stale, and verify all interface assumptions against deployed binaries per this repo's existing doctrine (D-031/D-032).
