# pump.fun bonding curve mechanics — exact detail (as of 2026-08)

Primary authoritative source: pump.fun's own reverse-engineering-friendly repo **pump-fun/pump-public-docs** (official), program `6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P` ("Pump"), AMM `pAMMBay6oceH9fJKBRHGP5D4bD4sWpmSwMn52FMfXEA` ("PumpSwap"), fee program `pfeeUxB6jkeY1Hxd7CsFCAjcbHA9rWtchMGdZ6VojVZ` ("Pump Fees").

## 1. Curve math (constant-product with virtual reserves)

Token: SPL mint, **1,000,000,000 tokens, 6 decimals** (total_supply stored as `1_000_000_000_000_000` base units); mint + freeze authority revoked at creation.

Global defaults (from official PUMP_PROGRAM_README):
- `initial_virtual_token_reserves = 1_073_000_000_000_000` (1.073B tokens)
- `initial_virtual_sol_reserves = 30_000_000_000` lamports (30 SOL)
- `initial_real_token_reserves = 793_100_000_000_000` (793.1M tokens actually for sale)
- `real_sol_reserves = 0` at creation; reserve of tokens NOT for sale on the curve = 1,000M − 793.1M = **206.9M tokens** (held for the graduation LP; equals virtual − real gap of 279.9M minus the 73M... — precisely: virtual−real token gap is 279.9M "virtual-only" tokens; the curve's token account holds 1000M, of which 793.1M sellable, 206.9M reserved for migration).
- `fee_basis_points = 100` (legacy flat 1%), `pool_migration_fee = 15_000_001` lamports.

Invariant: `virtual_sol_reserves × virtual_token_reserves = k`. Starting price = 30/1073e6 ≈ 2.796e-8 SOL/token; starting (fully-diluted) market cap = 30e9×1e15/1.073e15 ≈ **27.96 SOL**.

**Buy** (`buy(user, associated_user, mint, amount, max_sol_cost)` — exact-token-out): `sol_cost = k/(virtual_token − amount) − virtual_sol`; fee = sol_cost × (protocol_bps + creator_bps)/10_000, **charged on top** — only the net `sol_cost` enters both virtual and real SOL reserves; both virtual and real token reserves decrease by `amount`; tokens out are capped at remaining `real_token_reserves` (the last buy is truncated). The SDK's exact-SOL-in helper inverts this: `input = (sol−1)×10000/(fee_bps+10000)` then `tokensOut = input×vT/(vS+input)`.
**Sell** (`sell(..., amount, min_sol_output)`): gross `sol_out = virtual_sol − k/(virtual_token + amount)` (equivalently `amount×vS/(vT+amount)`), fee **deducted from proceeds** (ceil-rounded; clamped ≥0 for dust). Slippage protection is only `max_sol_cost` / `min_sol_output`.

Since Sept 2025 the trade instructions read a `FeeConfig` PDA (seeds `["fee_config", …]`, discriminator `[143,52,146,187,219,123,76,155]`) on the Pump Fees program; fee tier chosen by market cap = `virtual_sol × mint_supply / virtual_token` (lamports). Fees split protocol-recipients vs the per-creator `creator-vault` PDA.

## 2. Graduation / completion

- Completion condition: a buy exhausts `real_token_reserves == 0` → `complete = true`. Derived exactly from constants: SOL collected = k/279.9e12 − 30e9 = **85.005 SOL**; final virtual price ≈ 4.11e-7 SOL/token; completion market cap ≈ **410.9 SOL** (the "~$69k" of the 2024 era at SOL≈$168; USD figure floats with SOL — pump's own 2025 fee table labels the boundary "420 SOL ≈ $85k"). ~1.5% of launches ever graduate (odaily: 1.4%).
- **Raydium era (Jan 2024 – Mar 20 2025):** graduation was NOT permissionless. A now-disabled `withdraw` instruction let the `withdraw_authority` pubkey (Global config) pull the curve's SOL + 206.9M tokens; pump.fun's off-chain crank wallet `39azUYFWPz3VHgKCf3VChUwbpURdCHRxjWVowf5jUJjg` signed it, then created a Raydium AMM v4 pool and burned the LP tokens. Fee: **6 SOL** was skimmed, ~**79 SOL + ~206.9M tokens** seeded the Raydium pool (widely documented incl. The Block). CONFLICT to note: The Block's Aug 9 2024 piece says the migration fee then "stands at 1.5 SOL" (reduced from 2 SOL) funding a 0.5 SOL creator graduation reward, while The Block's own Mar 2025 PumpSwap piece and KuCoin/others say PumpSwap "eliminated the 6 SOL fee" — the fee amount clearly varied over 2024; treat "6 SOL for most of the era, reduced before removal" as the safe reading.
- **PumpSwap era (Mar 20 2025 → now):** `migrate` on the Pump program is **permissionless and idempotent** — anyone can crank a completed curve; it moves ALL curve liquidity (≈85 SOL minus `pool_migration_fee = 15_000_001` lamports ≈ 0.015 SOL, kept to cover account creation) plus the 206.9M reserved tokens into a new PumpSwap constant-product pool, mints LP and **immediately burns the LP** (liquidity locked forever). Migration is instant and free to the creator.

## 3. Fee structure history → current

- **Jan 2024 launch:** 1% flat swap fee on curve trades to protocol `fee_recipient`; token creation cost ~0.02 SOL paid by creator; 6 SOL Raydium migration fee.
- **Aug 9 2024:** creation fee shifted to the token's *first buyer* (creator deploys free); creators promised **0.5 SOL** on bonding-curve completion (The Block).
- **Mar 20 2025 — PumpSwap:** migration fee → 0; PumpSwap swap fee **0.25% = 0.20% LP + 0.05% protocol**.
- **May 12–13 2025 — creator fees v1:** Pump program upgraded (creator-vault PDA seeds `[b"creator-vault", bonding_curve.creator]`; `Global::creator_fee_basis_points` added; `BondingCurve` account extended to 150 bytes via `extend_account`; creator set via `create` arg, `set_metaplex_creator`, or admin `set_creator` backfill; `collect_creator_fee` signed by creator withdraws the vault). Creators earn **0.05%** of every trade (curve + PumpSwap); PumpSwap total fee rose to **0.30%** (0.20 LP / 0.05 protocol / 0.05 creator) — billed as "50% of protocol trading fee revenue" shared.
- **Sept 2–3 2025 — Project Ascend / Dynamic Fees V1** (official fees.png tier table, read directly from pump-public-docs): **Bonding curve: creator 0.30% + protocol 0.95% + LP 0% = 1.25% total (flat).** PumpSwap tiers by market cap (creator/protocol/LP/total): 0–420 SOL MC (≈$0–85k): 0.300/0.93/0.02 = 1.25%; 420–1470 SOL (≈$85k–300k): **0.950/0.05/0.20 = 1.20%**; 1470–2460: 0.900 → 1.15%; 2460–3440: 0.850 → 1.10%; 3440–4420: 0.800 → 1.05%; 4420–9820 (≈$900k–2M): 0.750 → 1.00%; 9820–14740: 0.700 → 0.95%; 14740–19650: 0.650 → 0.90%; 19650–24560: 0.600 → 0.85%; 24560–29470: 0.550 → 0.80%; 29470–34380: 0.500 → 0.75%; 34380–39300: 0.450 → 0.70%; 39300–44210: 0.400 → 0.65%; 44210–49120: 0.350 → 0.60%; 49120–54030: 0.300 → 0.55%; 54030–58940: 0.275 → 0.53%; 58940–63860: 0.250 → 0.50%; 63860–68770: 0.225 → 0.48%; 68770–73681: 0.200 → 0.45%; 73681–78590: 0.175 → 0.43%; 78590–83500: 0.150 → 0.40%; 83500–88400: 0.125 → 0.38%; 88400–93330: 0.100 → 0.35%; 93330–98240: 0.075 → 0.33%; **98240+ SOL (≈$20M+): 0.050/0.05/0.20 = 0.30%**. Protocol + LP shares unchanged above 420 SOL; the sub-420-SOL 0.93% protocol fee is explicitly an early-trading (anti-bot) surcharge that decays.
- **Jan 9 2026 — Creator Fee Sharing:** fees splittable across up to **10 wallets**; coin ownership transferable; update authority revocable; CTO admins can reassign; Alon: pivot toward "market-based"/trader-determined creator fees because deploy-and-farm was a "dangerous" low-risk incentive.
- **Feb 17 2026 — Cashback Coins:** at launch the creator irreversibly chooses Creator Fees OR Trader Cashback (100% of the 0.3% creator fee stream redirected to traders/holders; claimed via app `claim_cashback`; no CTO support).
- **Mar 2026:** post-launch creator-fee redirect limited to ONE change, then locked.
- **May 21–22 2026 — USDC quote pairs + v2:** `buy_v2` / `sell_v2` / `buy_exact_quote_in_v2` unify quote assets (SOL or USDC); new `Global::initial_virtual_quote_reserves`; PumpSwap pools gained a `virtual_quote_reserves` field (`effective_quote_reserves = pool_quote_token_account.amount + Pool::virtual_quote_reserves`); different MC thresholds for stable pairs; no extra cost for SOL pairs on v2. Since Aug 2025, protocol revenue funds PUMP buybacks (~1.36M SOL ≈ $236M through Jan 2026).

## 4. Anti-sniping / fair-launch / dev-buy

No structural anti-snipe mechanism exists in the program. "Fair launch" = no presale, no team allocation, everyone buys the same curve; creation is free (first buyer effectively pays); the frontend (and every bundler tool) lets the creator **bundle a dev-buy into the create transaction** (create + buy in one tx / Jito bundle). The `create` instruction doesn't even require the creator to sign as fee payer (enables "first buyer creates on behalf of creator"). Consequence: same-block sniping became dominant — a 2025 on-chain study found >50% of tokens sniped in the creation block, with deployer-funded hub-and-spoke sniper rings (~15,000 SOL/month extracted); arXiv 2607.02795 (2026) documents 1,012 persistent wallet rings. The only protocol-level countermeasure is economic: Dynamic Fees V1's elevated 1.25% early fee decaying with market cap, discussed as an anti-bot design.

## 5. Incidents

- **May 16 2024 insider flashloan drain (~12,300 SOL ≈ $1.9–2M):** ex-employee Jarett Dunn (@STACCoverflow) held/misappropriated the **withdraw authority** private key (the privileged key behind the Raydium migration `withdraw` instruction). He took SOL flash loans from **marginfi**, bought curves to 100% completion within the tx, then invoked `withdraw` to seize each completed curve's ~85 SOL liquidity, repaid the loans and kept the difference; airdropped some proceeds. ~$45M total curve TVL was at risk; pump.fun paused trading, upgraded the contract, and seeded affected curves with equal-or-greater liquidity within ~24h. Dunn was arrested in London May 18 2024 and later pleaded guilty (~$2M theft). This was **key/authority misuse, not curve-math failure** — and it's why the modern design deleted `withdraw` in favor of the permissionless `migrate`.
- **No public exploit of the constant-product math itself** has occurred; known griefs are economic (sniping, bundled dev-buys, deployer-funded rings). The rounding rules (fee ceil-round + zero-clamp on dust sells, buy truncation at real-reserve exhaustion) are the defensive edges reverse-engineered in SDK docs.
